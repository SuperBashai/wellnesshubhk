# WELL HK private-facilities audit — report source

Last checked: 2026-09-07

## Scope

Hong Kong SAR physical venues in Sports & fitness, Ice baths, Saunas, Healthy dining and Health shops. The audit prioritises operator pages and government facility records, keeps one listing per physical locality, and requires English and Traditional Chinese copy. Online-only shops, event-only recovery sessions and venues without a current verifiable address are excluded.

## Coverage result

- Existing directory before this audit: 189 unique venue pages.
- New verified cross-category records: 28.
- Private club access is labelled as members-only or restricted.
- Thrive Body belongs to Sports & fitness, Ice baths and Saunas.
- Sauna-equipped clubs are discoverable in both Sports & fitness and Saunas.
- Every new record has an operator URL, bilingual locality, bilingual description and bilingual address.

## Known gaps

This is not an exhaustive census. High-priority next passes: official GNC branch import, SaladStop and Heybo branches, independent healthy restaurants outside Central, hotel/spa saunas with day access, and additional private recreational lease holders. Residential clubhouses remain out of scope because access and current facility information are rarely public enough to verify responsibly.
