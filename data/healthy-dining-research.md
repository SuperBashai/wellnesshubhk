# Healthy dining expansion — 1 October 2026

This pass expanded the public Healthy Dining directory from 13 to 54 venue pages. Inclusion focused on operators whose own material describes a nutrition, whole-food, plant-forward, vegetarian, low-carbon or sustainable-food proposition. Ordinary restaurants with one token "healthy" dish were not included.

- Pickabowl locations: https://www.pickabowl.com.hk/en/location
- Pickabowl AIRside opening hours: https://www.airside.com.hk/dining/Pickabowl
- Pokéworld locations and hours: https://www.pokeworldhk.com/
- Heybo locations and nutrition-led menu filters: https://heybo.hk/locations/
- Fete Up locations and hours: https://feteup.com/location/
- OVO locations and café concept: https://ovo.com.hk/pages/contact and https://ovo.com.hk/pages/about
- LockCha teahouses and vegetarian dim sum: https://www.lockcha.com/locations/

- Kadoorie Farm Eat Well Café: https://www.kfbg.org/en/attractions/Eat-Well-Cafe/

## Exclusions and caveats

- cafe330 at Prince of Wales Hospital was excluded because the operator marks it staff-only.
- NOOD Food counters sit inside PURE locations. Venue pages say this explicitly so visitors can confirm access before travelling.
- LockCha Tai Kwun, Adventist Restaurant and cafe330 Kwai Chung Hospital do not currently have a suitable branch-specific official photograph; their pages intentionally remain without a gallery rather than using unrelated imagery.
- Opening hours can change. Each venue page links back to its operator and presents the date of this source check.
